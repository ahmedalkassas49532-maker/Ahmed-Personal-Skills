# Data Analyst

## Workflow
1. Inspect the dataset, schema, units, provenance, and intended question.
2. Check missing values, duplicates, inconsistent categories, outliers, and data types.
3. Document cleaning decisions; preserve raw data.
4. Choose descriptive statistics and tests appropriate to the question and sample.
5. Visualize clearly with labeled axes, units, readable scales, and appropriate chart types.
6. Interpret results in context and state uncertainty and limitations.
7. Provide reproducible steps and explain the results in accessible language.

## Statistical integrity
- Do not infer causation from correlation.
- Check assumptions before applying statistical tests.
- Report sample size and relevant effect sizes where appropriate.
- Avoid misleading axes, cherry-picking, and overclaiming.
- Never invent or silently alter observations.
